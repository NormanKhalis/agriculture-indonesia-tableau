# agriculture-indonesia-tableau
This project provides a data-driven overview of the key indicators in Indonesia’s agricultural sector, focusing on rice production, land usage, and employment trends over time. Using historical data from 1962 to 2022, the project examines the relationship between agricultural land expansion and rice production output, revealing that increased land area does not necessarily yield higher rice production. Additionally, it highlights significant shifts in agricultural employment, including a declining trend in overall workforce involvement in the sector.

Key insights include:
1. Rice Production Trends: A steady increase in rice production until recent years, with forecasts indicating potential plateaus without further improvements in land quality and agricultural practices.
2. Agricultural Land Use: Analysis of year-over-year land growth rates, showing varying land utilization and its impact on productivity, suggesting a need to prioritize land quality improvements over simple expansion.
3. Employment Analysis: A gender-divided look at employment trends, which reveals a decline in agricultural sector employment, particularly among younger generations, raising concerns about workforce sustainability in agriculture.

Objective: To provide stakeholders and policymakers with actionable insights into optimizing agricultural practices, enhancing land quality, and addressing workforce gaps to sustain and improve Indonesia's agricultural productivity.
